Before your first conversation

Audio is transcribed locally by Whisper in your browser. Text is sent to MyMemory and, when connected, TypeSafe. Do not enter confidential information. No microphone is activated until you press Start speaking.

Local speech works in modern browsers on localhost or HTTPS. The model is downloaded once and cached. Available voices depend on your device. Jev confidence is not a guarantee of translation accuracy.
