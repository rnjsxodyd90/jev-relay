# Jev Relay

English to Dutch, with context.

Jev Relay is a native iPhone and iPad conversation assistant. Prepare your English text, add the context that matters, and review the Dutch wording before you play it aloud.

## You choose what happens next

- **Type or dictate.** Optional speech recognition runs on the device when the required language support is available. You can always type instead.
- **Add context and tone.** Clarify who you are speaking to and what you mean.
- **Review before speaking.** Playback starts only when you choose it, using an available Dutch voice.
- **Keep useful phrases.** The phrasebook works locally; you choose which additional phrases to save or delete.

Live interpretation needs an internet connection and your permission to send text to the named AI services. Daily and shared service limits apply. The local phrasebook remains available without live interpretation.

Jev makes several routing decisions together. New wording is generated only when needed. These tools can make mistakes; this is not a certified translator or a service for legal, medical, financial, or emergency decisions.
